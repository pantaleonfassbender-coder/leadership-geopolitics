Rights. Every text on this site is in the United States public domain: the original English works by age (Bacon 1625, Hobbes 1651, Mun 1664, Smith 1776, Mahan 1890, Mackinder 1904), and the remainder in public-domain translations named, edition by edition, on the method page (Crawley 1874, Giles 1910, Shamasastry 1915, Marriott 1908, Magoffin 1916, Graham 1873). The site takes its name from Cicero's historia magistra vitae — history, the teacher of life — a claim it quotes with the reservations set out in the introduction. The corpus ends, deliberately, at 1904: the strategic canon of the century that followed — Schelling, Liddell Hart, Wohlstetter — remains in copyright and outside this site's rules.
